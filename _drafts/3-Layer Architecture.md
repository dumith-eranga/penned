A building has 3 main parts
- the foundation holds everything together
- the roof protect you from the rain and sun
- the walls provide structure for the intended purpose
    - a church or a ballroom has a massive single space for people to gather
    - a house has rooms for different functions ar personal level: living, sleeping, laundry
    - an office has rooms or areas for group functions : meetings, lunch, team cubicles


Software architecture follow a similar pattern
- the framework of the system (not a specific language framework) hold things together
- guards protect you from the elements
    - input validation and sanitisation
    - rate-limiting and firewall rules
- the business logic and interfaces
