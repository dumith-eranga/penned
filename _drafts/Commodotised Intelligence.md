AI commodotised intelligence.

In constrast to what some say on social media, it did not turn a marketer to a software developer - it simply lowered the barrier to entry. Just like a marketer can quickly whip up a software product or a service, a developer can conjure a marketing strategy and a plan. When you want to scale beyond the initial few customers the finess and the tacit knowledge of the respective field comes nigh.

Think of AI like money - it amplifies what you already are by augmenting you.

A hoarder becomes an even bigger hoarder when they have money. A person who wants to help people gets to do more so when they have money.
