Model, Harness, and Agent are three words that encounter often in the context of Artificial Intelligence. Understanding the differences and how they relate helps one become a better user of AI.


A **model** is a software module that generate an output through statistical computation based on a given input.
An analogy is an engine. It takes fuel in and convert that to mechanical energy.
It is the processor that output raw power.


A **harness** is a collection of software tools that are used to achieve specific outcomes.
One can think of this like a car. An engine by itself is not very useful in general. It needs several other systems that come together to form a car that can take a person from A to B.
While the whole thing is now useful, one still needs to drive it to get from A to B.


An **agent** is a software that simulate agency.
It is similar to a taxi for hire. It comes with an engine, a car, and a person who drives it.
Now, the passenger only need to state the intent - destination in this case. The agency of the driver takes care of the rest.


Each layer powers the layer above and is dependent on the layer below.
